# Feedback Session Mode

For sessions where someone with more context reviews work in progress and gives directional feedback: a mentor, an advisor, a manager, a stakeholder or a client.

## Template

```markdown
# {Project} - Feedback Session with {Name}
**Date:** {Date}
**Participants:** {Team members present}, {Reviewer + role}
**Format:** {Scheduled review / check-in / milestone review / client walkthrough}
**Stage:** {Where the work is - early draft, pre-launch, post-pilot, etc.}

---

## What we showed / where we are

{Brief statement of what the team brought to the session. Their current thesis, draft, or framing.}

---

## Feedback received

### Positive signals
{What the reviewer endorsed, validated, or expressed enthusiasm for. Important - the team needs to know what to protect.}

- {Endorsement 1} - direct framing: "{quote if available}"
- {Endorsement 2}

### Critical feedback
{What the reviewer pushed back on, questioned, or flagged as weak. Preserve their phrasing.}

- {Critique 1} - "{quote}"
- {Critique 2}

### Gaps flagged
{Things the reviewer said are missing - analysis not done, evidence not gathered, questions not answered.}

- {Gap 1}
- {Gap 2}

---

## Direction shifts implied

{If the feedback implies the team should change direction - pivot the thesis, drop a section, reframe the segment - capture it here. Make it actionable.}

---

## What to fix before the next milestone

| Priority | Fix | What it requires |
|---|---|---|
| High | {What needs fixing} | {Research / rework / new evidence} |
| Medium | {Next priority} | {} |
| Low | {Nice-to-have} | {} |

---

## Phrases to remember

{Specific phrases the reviewer uses repeatedly. These are anchors - they signal what this person weighs when they judge the work. Examples: "diagnosis over description," "compliance ceiling."}

---

## Open questions for the next check-in

{Things the team didn't get answered, or things the reviewer asked the team to come back with.}

---

## Attribution questions

{Usually clean in this mode - one reviewer voice + team. But flag if team-side attribution is unclear.}
```

## Mode-specific rules

- **Preserve the reviewer's framings verbatim.** People repeat the phrases that carry their priorities. Catch them and quote them precisely - they tell the team what will win the next review.
- **Separate praise from critique.** Don't soften critique by burying it under positives. The critical feedback is the most actionable part of the session - make it findable.
- **Convert feedback into fixes.** A feedback session only matters if the team acts on it. The "What to fix" table is the bridge from feedback to the next milestone.
- **Capture direction shifts explicitly.** Reviewers often suggest pivots without naming them as such ("have you considered..."). If the suggestion implies redirecting work, name the shift in "Direction shifts implied."
- **Tone matters.** A "have you thought about X?" delivered casually is different from "you really need to address X." Note tone if it's directional.

## Naming

`{Project}_{Reviewer}_Feedback_{Date}.md`

Examples:
- `PricingPilot_Advisor_Feedback_Apr17.md`
- `Onboarding_ClientWalkthrough_May14.md`
- `Q3Plan_ManagerReview_Jun02.md`
