# Mentoring Session Mode

For faculty feedback meetings, advisor sessions, professor office hours, or any session where an expert is reviewing student work and giving directional feedback.

Gold standard: `MentoringSession_Class4_Apr17.md`, `MT_Bank_Professor_Feedback.md`, `ProfessorOtherTeam_Discussion_Notes.md`.

## Template

```markdown
# {Project} - Mentoring Session with {Faculty / Advisor Name}
**Date:** {Date}
**Participants:** {Team members present}, {Faculty member + role}
**Format:** {Office hours / scheduled feedback / mid-deliverable check-in}
**Stage:** {Where the team is - pre-D2 draft, post-D3 submission, etc.}

---

## What we showed / where we are

{Brief statement of what the team brought to the session. Their current thesis, draft, or framing.}

---

## Feedback received

### Positive signals
{What the faculty member endorsed, validated, or expressed enthusiasm for. Important - the user's team needs to know what to protect.}

- {Endorsement 1} - direct framing: "{quote if available}"
- {Endorsement 2}

### Critical feedback
{What the faculty member pushed back on, questioned, or flagged as weak. Preserve their phrasing.}

- {Critique 1} - "{quote}"
- {Critique 2}

### Gaps flagged
{Things the faculty member said are missing - analysis the team hasn't done, evidence not gathered, questions not answered.}

- {Gap 1}
- {Gap 2}

---

## Direction shifts implied

{If the feedback implies the team should change direction - pivot the thesis, drop a section, reframe the segment - capture it here. Make it actionable.}

---

## What to fix before next deliverable

| Priority | Fix | What it requires |
|---|---|---|
| High | {What needs fixing} | {Research / rework / new evidence} |
| Medium | {Next priority} | {} |
| Low | {Nice-to-have} | {} |

---

## Faculty-specific framings to remember

{Specific phrases the faculty member uses repeatedly. These are anchors - they signal what the faculty member prioritizes when grading. Examples: "diagnosis over description," "compliance ceiling."}

---

## Open questions for next check-in

{Things the team didn't get answered, or things the faculty member said to come back with.}

---

## Attribution questions

{Usually clean in this mode - one faculty voice + team. But flag if team-side attribution is unclear.}
```

## Mode-specific rules

- **Preserve faculty framings verbatim.** Faculty repeat phrases. Those phrases are how they grade. Catch them - "diagnosis over description," "panopticon model," "Empowered Representative model," "compliance ceiling." Quote them precisely.
- **Separate praise from critique.** Don't soften critique by burying it under positives. The critical feedback is the most actionable part of the session - make it findable.
- **Convert feedback into fixes.** Mentoring sessions only matter if the team acts on them. The "What to fix" table is the bridge from feedback to next deliverable.
- **Capture direction shifts explicitly.** Faculty often suggest pivots without naming them as such ("have you considered..."). If the suggestion implies redirecting work, name the shift in "Direction shifts implied."
- **Tone of faculty matters.** A "have you thought about X?" delivered casually is different from "you really need to address X." Note tone if it's directional.
- **Cross-team intelligence.** If the session includes the faculty member discussing other teams' work (`ProfessorOtherTeam_Discussion_Notes.md`), capture that separately - it tells the team what the faculty member sees as the competitive landscape.

## Naming

`{Project}_{Faculty_Name_or_Generic}_Feedback_{Date}.md`
or `MentoringSession_{Context}_{Date}.md`

Examples:
- `MT_Bank_Professor_Feedback.md`
- `MentoringSession_Class4_Apr17.md`
- `Strategy_Smith_OfficeHours_May14.md`
